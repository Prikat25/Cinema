TITLE: THE CYBERNETIC COURIER
WRITTEN BY: ALEX RIVERS

EXT. NEO-TOKYO ALLEYWAY - DUSK

Neon signs flicker against wet asphalt. Rain pours relentlessly.

NEO (30s), wearing a reflective cybernetic jacket and carrying a glowing chrome briefcase, sprint down the narrow alley.

He glances over his shoulder. Two ENFORCERS in heavy tactical armor pursue with plasma batons raised.

NEO
(into wrist comm)
Kira, I've got heat! Unlock the dock door now!

INT. CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

KIRA (20s), wearing futuristic comm-headset and dark tech-visor, frantically taps away at a holographic keyboard. Multiple video feeds float before her.

KIRA
Door 4 is open, Neo! Watch the drone above you!

EXT. NEO-TOKYO ALLEYWAY - CONTINUOUS

Neo slides across the bonnet of a parked hover-car. A SURVEILLANCE DRONE descends from above, spotlight pinning Neo against the brick wall.

Neo draws an EMP DISRUPTOR from his holster, aims, and fires a pulsing blue wave.

The drone sparks, short-circuits, and crashes into the alley trash cans.

NEO
Door reached!
